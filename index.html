Concept trends

Is your concept rising or fading?

Every concept, with two independent signals: venues actually recorded in London over time (category level, from OpenStreetMap history) and what people search for (Google Trends). Sort by growth to spot the risers.

Honest labels: the venue series counts every recorded venue in the concept's category (all cafés, all restaurants...) in the OpenStreetMap extract history - mapper coverage grows over time, so some of the rise is better mapping, not just more openings; it is OBSERVED data with that caveat. The search series is Google Trends interest for a matching keyword, an ESTIMATED index of attention, not shops or sales. Concept-level shop counts per year are not published by any open source at this granularity - where a figure is not available, the row says so instead of guessing.
