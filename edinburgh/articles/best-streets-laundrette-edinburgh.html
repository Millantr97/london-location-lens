The 10 best streets to open a laundrette or dry cleaner in Edinburgh (2026 data)

Laundrettes are a pure neighbourhood business: dense renters, few cars, weekly habits. The model scores exactly that - residents within walking distance and passing flow. These ten streets scored highest.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 249 Edinburgh street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits (National Rail: ORR Estimates of Station Usage 2024-25 (annual; day-of-week split modelled)), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2022 (Scotland) residents for the local Data Zone, and rent context from the Scottish Assessors valuation roll. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
