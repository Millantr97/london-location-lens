Concept trends

Is your concept rising or fading?

Every concept, led by what people actually search for (Google Trends, UK, 2021-2026), with the venues recorded today as supply context (OpenStreetMap). Sort by rising interest to spot the risers, or search for a concept.

Honest labels: the search series is Google Trends interest for a matching keyword (United Kingdom, yearly mean), an ESTIMATED index of attention - not shops or sales. The supply figure counts every venue currently recorded in the concept's category in OpenStreetMap - OBSERVED, a snapshot of today. We deliberately show no venue history: mapped coverage grew several-fold over 2017-2026, faster than any real market, so year-by-year venue counts would measure mapping progress, not openings - and no open source publishes concept-level shop counts per year at this granularity.
