Commercial location intelligence · Zones 1-3

Know the street before you sign the lease.

Compare London micro-areas for a specific business. Location Lens combines street form, transport demand, local population, competition, property evidence and public realm - while showing exactly what each number can and cannot tell you.

Built to avoid false precision.

People are not tracked. Demographics describe aggregated residential areas, not individual passers-by. Station demand is not street footfall. Modelled scores are labelled and never presented as observed facts.

Evidence, not surveillance

Every signal carries its own resolution.

OBSERVED Direct evidence

Mapped streets, parks, entrances, business premises, station entry/exit totals and administrative property records. The tool keeps the native geography and date.

  • OS OpenData
  • TfL network demand
  • VOA rateable values

AREA CONTEXT Aggregated context

Age, sex, country of birth, occupation and employment are only shown at the smallest defensible published Census geography. They do not identify or infer any person.

  • ONS Census geographies
  • Occupation, Census 2021
  • Country of birth, Census 2021

MODELLED Transparent proxy

Walk-time catchments, daypart opportunity and suitability scores combine evidence with stated weights. They are decision aids, not forecasts. Paid mobility can improve footfall where licensed.

  • High Streets open data
  • Subscriber mobility data
  • Approximate street crime data
