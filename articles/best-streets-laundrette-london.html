The 10 best streets to open a laundrette or dry cleaner in London (2026 data)

Laundrettes are a pure neighbourhood business: dense renters, few cars, weekly habits. The model scores exactly that - residents within walking distance and passing flow. These ten streets scored highest.

Format scored: A self-service laundrette with service washes: £7 average ticket, 12 machines, open seven days including evenings.

Computed 12 September 2026 over 2,480 London street segments. Every figure carries its label: OBSERVED, AREA CONTEXT or MODELLED.

Read the labels, not just the score

OBSERVED measured at the street or station. AREA CONTEXT residents or borough statistics around it. MODELLED transparent estimates we compute, with the rule shown. Nothing is presented as more precise than its source.

How this ranking works

One fixed concept is scored against every one of the 2,480 London street segments in the Location Potential dataset: station entries and exits from TfL Annual Station Counts 2025 (National Rail: ORR 2024-25), recorded venues within the concept's competition radius (400 m to 1 km by type) from OpenStreetMap, Census 2021 residents for the local LSOA, business crime from data.police.uk, and rent context from Valuation Office Agency rateable values. Scores and revenue figures are MODELLED planning estimates with fixed, published rules, not observed takings. The full method, sources and limits are on the Method page.
